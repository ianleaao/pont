Show steps, fees and official links only. No legal advice. Fees change: always show source and date checked.
Amounts and sources live in data/fees.json and data/colleges.json. This doc must match them.

## Visa situations (onboarding question 6)
| Situation | Can study? | Pont shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| U.S. citizen or permanent resident | Yes | No visa steps; FAFSA path |
| No visa, starting from zero | After F-1, M-1 or J-1 | Full checklist below |
| Already F-1 at another school | Yes, full-time, 12 credits | SEVIS transfer checklist; no federal aid |
| Tourist visa (B-1, B-2) or ESTA | No course of study | ESTA: leave and apply from home. B-1/B-2: change of status (I-539) or apply from home |
| H-4, L-2, E or G dependent | Yes, full or part-time | No visa change needed; optional F-1 switch |
| F-2 dependent | Only part-time recreational | Switch to F-1 for a degree. Its own situation in the engine (`f2_dependent`, routing only, never folded into the dependent visas); the plan shows the switch to F-1 steps with official links |
| DACA or no status | Can enroll; no FAFSA | College aid, official guidance only |

Program type: degree = F-1 (college issues I-20); vocational = M-1 (I-20); exchange = J-1 (sponsor issues DS-2019).

## Checklist: starting from zero (main user)
1. Valid passport.
2. English test and SAT or ACT if required (UT requires an official SAT or ACT).
3. Pont shows the full cost.
4. Apply and pay the application fee. UT fall: Early Action 15 Oct, Regular 1 Dec, decisions 15 Jan or 15 Feb.
5. After admission, request the I-20 in the college portal. Upload proof of funds (bank letter or statement, signed, dated, currency shown, usually within 3 to 6 months), sponsor form if parents pay, passport copy. Non-English documents: UT accepts a separate signed translation; other colleges may require a certified translation. UT does not accept documents by mail, email or fax.
6. College issues Form I-20.
7. Pay SEVIS I-901.
8. DS-160 with photo; pay the visa fee.
9. Book the consulate interview. F and M visas can be issued up to 365 days before the program start.
10. Interview: passport, signed I-20, SEVIS receipt, DS-160 confirmation, admission letter, financial proof, school records. Social media profiles must be public for F, M and J applicants.
11. If approved: Visa Integrity Fee where collected, reciprocity fee if any.
12. Enter the U.S. no earlier than 30 days before the program start.
13. Report to the college international office.
14. If refused: strengthen and reapply (new DS-160 and fee), defer, or other options.

## Checklist: F-1 transfer
1. Admitted by the new college. 2. Current school releases the SEVIS record on a release date. 3. New college issues a transfer I-20, same SEVIS ID, no new SEVIS fee. 4. Valid F-1 visa still usable. 5. Report within 15 days of the program start.

## Checklist: change to F-1 inside the U.S.
1. ESTA entrants cannot change status inside the U.S. 2. Admission and I-20. 3. SEVIS fee. 4. USCIS Form I-539. 5. B-1/B-2 holders cannot start classes until approved.

## Fees by phase (example: Lucas, Brazil, UT Austin CS)
Phase 1, before applying: passport (home country), SAT abroad: Not confirmed (collegeboard.org), TOEFL iBT in Brazil: Not confirmed (ets.org), Duolingo English Test: Not confirmed (englishtest.duolingo.com), UT application 90 USD (no waivers for internationals). Not confirmed test fees are shown with the official link and are not added to the total.
Phase 2, after admission: enrollment deposit 200 USD (credited to tuition), SEVIS 350 USD (ice.gov I-901 page), visa 185 USD (travel.state.gov visa fees), both checked 10 Nov 2026 (official pages blocked automated access; confirmed via sources citing them), Visa Integrity Fee 250 USD where collected (Brazil: Not confirmed, not added), reciprocity varies, consulate trip, flight, housing deposit. SHOW for I-20: UT publishes 71,912 USD for 2026-27 (undergraduate, nonresident; see docs/calculation-rules.md section 5).
Phase 3, first semester: cost of attendance items, ISSS Support Services Fee 225 USD per semester (450 USD per year; global.utexas.edu/isss/about/fees), UT student health insurance, automatic for F and J (2026-27, global.utexas.edu/isss/advising-services/insurance/costs): first year at UT 4,217.85 USD (New Student Fall 324.45 + Fall 1,297.80 + Spring and Summer 2,595.60); other years 3,893.40 USD. In the ACC path, year 3 is the first year at UT.
Phase 4, every year: repeats with increases and exchange rate; visa renewal only if it expires and the student travels; rule-change risk (USCIS I-539 extension if the end of duration of status returns; blocked by a federal court on 14 Sep 2026).
Phase 5, after graduation: OPT I-765 470 USD online or 520 paper (premium processing 1,780 USD, optional); UT OPT admin fee 150 USD (STEM extension 300 USD).
USCIS fees (I-539 420 online / 470 paper, premium 2,075; I-765 470 online / 520 paper, premium 1,780): uscis.gov G-1055, edition 10/16/26, checked 10 Nov 2026. The 16 Oct 2026 inflation adjustment changed only asylum, parole and TPS fees.

## Rule-change alerts (show as alert banners)
- Duration of status rule: final rule published, blocked nationwide by a federal court on 14 Sep 2026; litigation continues.
- Visa Integrity Fee: law; collection varies by country.
- Travel restrictions: additional countries restricted since Dec 2025; check by nationality.
